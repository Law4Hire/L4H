# Law4Hire Service-Fit Assessment (Knock-Out Tree)

This assessment determines if a user is a candidate for our services. Every path must end in "GOOD FIT FOR SERVICE" or "TERMINATED" (Not a candidate).

## Q1: What is the primary type of service or status you are seeking?

### Answer: Immigrant Visa (Permanent Residency / Green Card)
#### Q2: Do you have a qualifying basis for immigration (U.S. family member, U.S. job offer, or $800k+ to invest)?
##### Answer: Yes
###### Q3: Are you a citizen of a country currently subject to a total U.S. administrative processing hold (e.g., Algeria)?
*   **Yes:** Terminated (Country-wide hold prevents processing)
*   **No:** Q4: Have you ever been deported from the U.S. or stayed past your visa expiry for more than 1 year?
    *   **Yes:** Terminated (Requires advanced litigation outside standard service scope)
    *   **No:** Q5: Have you ever been convicted of a felony or a "Crime Involving Moral Turpitude"?
        *   **Yes:** Terminated
        *   **No:** **RESULT: GOOD FIT FOR SERVICE**
##### Answer: No (Seeking self-sponsorship)
###### Q3: Do you possess "Extraordinary Ability" or are you a person of "National Interest" to the U.S.?
*   **Yes:** Q4: Can you provide documentation of national or international awards/recognition?
    *   **Yes:** Q5: Are you a citizen of a country currently under administrative hold?
        *   **Yes:** Terminated
        *   **No:** **RESULT: GOOD FIT FOR SERVICE**
    *   **No:** Terminated (Self-sponsorship requires specific high-level criteria)
*   **No:** Terminated (Permanent residency requires a specific sponsor or high-level skill)
