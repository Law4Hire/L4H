# Group 3 Knock-Out & Dependency Tree: Green Card Adjustment & Maintenance

This document defines the strict eligibility checks and mandatory form dependencies for Group 3 USCIS forms.

---

## 1. I-485: Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status
**Goal:** Apply for a Green Card from inside the U.S.

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Are you currently physically present inside the United States?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Must use Consular Processing/DS-260 from abroad)
*   **Q2: Did you enter the U.S. with legal inspection (valid visa, parole, or border crossing card)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Unless qualifying under Section 245(i) - see I-485A)
*   **Q3: Do you have an approved or concurrently filed underlying petition (e.g., I-130, I-140, I-360)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q4
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (An "anchor" petition is required for adjustment)
*   **Q4: Is a visa number currently available to you in the Visa Bulletin (or are you an "Immediate Relative")?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Must wait until priority date is current)

### Mandatory Dependencies:
*   **I-693** (Report of Medical Examination) - Required for all I-485 applicants.
*   **I-864** (Affidavit of Support) - Required for most family-based and some employment-based cases.
*   **I-765** (Work Permit) - Optional but highly recommended (bundled dependency).
*   **I-131** (Travel Document) - Optional but highly recommended (bundled dependency).

---

## 2. I-485A: Supplement A to Form I-485 (Section 245(i))
**Goal:** Adjust status despite unlawful entry or status violations.

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Are you the beneficiary of an immigrant petition (I-130/I-140) or labor cert filed on or before April 30, 2001?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (245(i) protection only applies to pre-2001 filings)
*   **Q2: Were you physically present in the U.S. on December 21, 2000?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Requirement for petitions filed between 1998 and 2001)

---

## 3. I-485J: Confirmation of Valid Job Offer / Job Portability
**Goal:** Confirm job offer for I-485 or switch employers (portability).

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Is your I-485 based on an employment-based petition (I-140)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (I-485J is only for employment-based adjustment)
*   **Q2: Has your I-485 been pending for at least 180 days?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE** (Portability path)
    *   No: **ELIGIBLE** (Only if this is the initial filing for a non-concurrent case)

---

## 4. I-693: Report of Medical Examination and Vaccination Record
**Goal:** Provide medical clearance for I-485.

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Are you currently applying for or have a pending Adjustment of Status (I-485)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Medical exams are only for final status adjustment)
*   **Q2: Will the exam be performed by a USCIS-designated Civil Surgeon?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Results from non-designated doctors are invalid)

---

## 5. I-698: Application to Adjust Status from Temporary to Permanent Resident
**Goal:** Adjust status for specialized groups (e.g., certain legalized aliens).

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Were you granted temporary resident status under Section 245A or 210 of the INA?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (This form is restricted to specific legacy groups)

---

## 6. I-751: Petition to Remove Conditions on Residence
**Goal:** Convert a 2-year conditional Green Card to a 10-year permanent card (Marriage-based).

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Do you currently have a conditional Green Card valid for 2 years?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Use Form I-90 for 10-year card renewals)
*   **Q2: Is your card expiring within the next 90 days?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Filing too early - USCIS will reject unless requesting a waiver)
*   **Q3: Are you filing jointly with your U.S. citizen spouse?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No (Divorced/Deceased/Abused): **ELIGIBLE** (Requires a "Waiver of the Joint Filing Requirement")

---

## 7. I-829: Petition by Investor to Remove Conditions on Permanent Resident Status
**Goal:** Convert a 2-year conditional Green Card to permanent (EB-5 Investor).

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Did you obtain your conditional Green Card through an EB-5 investment?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Use I-751 for marriage-based conditions)
*   **Q2: Have you maintained your investment "at risk" throughout the 2-year period?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Disqualified from removing conditions)
*   **Q3: Has your investment created at least 10 full-time jobs for U.S. workers?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**

---

## 8. I-90: Application to Replace Permanent Resident Card
**Goal:** Renew or replace a 10-year Green Card.

### Knock-Out Questions:
*   **Q1: Are you a Lawful Permanent Resident?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q2: Is your current card a 10-year card (not a 2-year conditional card)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Must file I-751 or I-829)
*   **Q3: Is your card expired, expiring within 6 months, or lost/stolen?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Filing more than 6 months before expiry is prohibited)
