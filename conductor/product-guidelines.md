# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice and Tone
- **Modern & Approachable:** The language should be clear, friendly, and accessible. Avoid overly dense legal jargon when communicating with clients. Focus on making the legal process feel manageable and transparent.

## UX & UI Principles
- **Minimalist & Clean:** Interfaces should be free of clutter, utilizing ample whitespace to guide the user's focus to key actions.
- **Step-by-Step Wizards:** Complex processes (like intake forms or document uploads) should be broken down into small, digestible, and sequential steps to reduce cognitive load.

## Accessibility
- **Strict WCAG Compliance:** Adhere to WCAG AA/AAA standards to ensure the platform is accessible to users with disabilities, including proper contrast, semantic HTML, and screen reader compatibility.

## Layout Strategy
- **Desktop-First:** Optimize views and data density for large screens first, ensuring that power users (lawyers and admins) have the tools they need. Interfaces must still be fully responsive and functional on mobile devices, scaled down appropriately.
