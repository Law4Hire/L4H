# Initial Concept
Modern legal services platform.

# Product Definition

## Vision
To provide a modern, secure, and comprehensive legal services platform that connects lawyers, legal clients, and platform administrators seamlessly. The platform aims to modernize legal practice by streamlining workflows and reducing manual overhead.

## Target Audience
- **Lawyers/Firms:** Attorneys and legal staff managing cases, documents, and client communication.
- **Legal Clients:** Individuals or businesses seeking legal assistance, submitting intake forms, and tracking case progress.
- **Platform Admins:** System administrators responsible for managing the platform, user roles, and billing configurations.

## Core Features
- **Client Portal & Intake:** Streamlined intake forms, questionnaires, and client profile management to simplify onboarding.
- **Document Management:** Secure upload, generation, and sharing of legal documents between clients and attorneys.
- **Case Tracking:** Milestone tracking, task assignment, and status updates for transparent case management.

## Pain Points Solved
- **Manual Data Entry:** Replaces repetitive manual data collection with streamlined onboarding and digital form collection.
- **Client Visibility:** Provides self-serve portals allowing clients to check their case status at any time, improving transparency and trust.

## Key Integrations
- **Payment Gateways:** Integration with providers like Stripe for handling invoicing, retainers, and billing.
- **E-signatures:** Integration with services like DocuSign to facilitate legally binding digital agreements.
- **Calendar Sync:** Synchronization with Outlook or Google Calendar for scheduling consultations and managing attorney availability.
