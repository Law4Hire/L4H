# Document Eligibility Knock-Out Tree (Comprehensive USCIS List)

This document provides the qualifying questions for all USCIS forms currently supported by Law4Hire. To maintain clarity across 100+ documents, forms are grouped by their primary legal function.

---

## 1. Nonimmigrant Visa Applications (Abroad)
*Includes: DS-156, DS-156E, DS-160, DS-230, DS-260, DS-261*

*   **Q1: Are you currently located outside the United States?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Use I-539 for status changes inside the U.S.)
*   **Q2: Do you have a specific purpose for your visit (Tourism, Work, Study, or Investment)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Valid intent is a prerequisite)
*   **Q3: Have you been issued a qualifying prerequisite document (e.g., I-20 for students, I-797 for workers)?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Prerequisites must be met before filing)

---

## 2. Family-Based Petitions & Adoptions
*Includes: I-130, I-129F, I-600, I-600A, I-730, I-800, I-800A, I-864, I-864A, I-864EZ, I-864W*

*   **Q1: Are you a U.S. Citizen or Lawful Permanent Resident (Petitioner)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q2: Is the beneficiary a qualifying relative (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, or Fiancé)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q3: (If Adoption) Has the child been legally determined to be an orphan or from a Convention country?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**

---

## 3. Employment-Based Petitions
*Includes: I-129, I-129CW, I-129CWR, I-129S, I-140, I-140G, I-905, I-907*

*   **Q1: Are you a U.S. Employer, Agent, or an individual with Extraordinary Ability?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Individual workers cannot self-petition for standard I-129/I-140)
*   **Q2: Does the position meet the specific educational or skill requirements for the category (e.g., Bachelor's for H-1B)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q3: Is the employer willing to guarantee payment of the prevailing wage?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**

---

## 4. Green Card Adjustment & Maintenance
*Includes: I-485, I-485A, I-485J, I-90, I-751, I-829, I-693, I-698*

*   **Q1: (For I-485) Are you currently physically present in the U.S. and entered with inspection?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Consular processing required)
*   **Q2: (For I-90/I-751) Are you a current Permanent Resident (Green Card holder)?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q3: Is your card expiring within 6 months, or do you have a qualifying life event (Marriage termination for I-751)?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**

---

## 5. Naturalization & Citizenship
*Includes: N-300, N-336, N-400, N-426, N-470, N-565, N-600, N-600K, N-644, N-648*

*   **Q1: Are you a Lawful Permanent Resident?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Prerequisite for N-400)
*   **Q2: Have you met the continuous residence (5 years / 3 years) and physical presence requirements?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q3
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q3: Can you demonstrate Good Moral Character (no disqualifying criminal record)?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**

---

## 6. Humanitarian, Asylum & Protection
*Includes: I-589, I-817, I-821, I-821D, I-854, I-881, I-914, I-918, I-929*

*   **Q1: Do you fear persecution or have you been a victim of a crime/trafficking?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Specific humanitarian basis required)
*   **Q2: (For Asylum) Have you been in the U.S. for less than one year?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Unless an extraordinary exception applies)

---

## 7. Investor Programs
*Includes: I-526, I-526E, I-924, I-924A, I-941, I-956, I-956F, I-956G, I-956H, I-956K*

*   **Q1: Do you have at least $800,000 available for investment in a U.S. enterprise?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
*   **Q2: Can you document the lawful source of the entire investment capital?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**

---

## 8. Waivers & Appeals
*Includes: I-191, I-192, I-193, I-212, I-290B, I-601, I-601A, I-612, I-690, I-694*

*   **Q1: Have you been found inadmissible to the United States or had an application denied?**
    *   Yes: Continue to Q2
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Waivers/Appeals require a prior negative determination)
*   **Q2: Can you demonstrate "Extreme Hardship" to a qualifying U.S. relative?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED** (Standard for most I-601/I-601A waivers)

---

## 9. Administrative & Status Maintenance
*Includes: AR-11, G-28, G-28I, G-325A, G-325R, G-639, G-845, G-884, G-1041, G-1041A, G-1145, G-1256, G-1450, G-1566, I-9, I-102, I-134, I-539, I-566, I-824, I-865, I-910, I-912, I-945*

*   **Q1: Do you have an active immigration case or status that requires an update (address change, fee waiver, duplicate document)?**
    *   Yes: **ELIGIBLE**
    *   No: **TERMINATED**
